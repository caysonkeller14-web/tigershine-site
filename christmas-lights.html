Good to know

Christmas light FAQ

How much does Christmas light installation cost?

It depends on your house — how much roofline, how many gables and peaks, and whether you want wreaths, garland, or shrubs and trees lit. Every quote is free. Call or text 334-542-4044, or send a photo of the front of your house and we'll come back with a design and a price.

When should I book?

As early as you can. Installation weekends fill up fast, and the earliest bookings get first pick of dates. Booking opens in the fall.

Do I have to buy the lights?

No. We supply the lights, clips, and timers, design the layout for your house, and install everything. You never buy, tangle, or store a box of lights.

Is takedown included?

Yes, with every job. We come back in January, take everything down, and haul it away — no extra call and no extra charge.

What if a strand or bulb goes out during the season?

Text us. In-season fixes are part of the service, so we come back and get it lit again.

Do you hang lights at Lake Martin?

Yes. We serve Lake Martin along with Auburn, Opelika, and the surrounding area, including Columbus, GA and Montgomery. If you're just outside that, call anyway — we often can.

Do you use subcontractors?

No. We're a small local crew based in Auburn. The two of us design, hang, and take down every job ourselves.
