Frequently asked questions

Will pressure washing damage my concrete, siding, or roof?

No — because we match the method to the surface. Concrete and other hard flatwork get a surface cleaner at an appropriate pressure. Siding, brick, painted surfaces, and roofs get low-pressure soft washing, where a cleaning solution does the work instead of force.

What's the difference between pressure washing and soft washing?

Pressure washing uses water force and is right for concrete and masonry. Soft washing uses low pressure plus a cleaning solution and is right for anything that force could damage — siding, screens, wood, and roofs. Most homes need both, on different surfaces.

Do you need my water or power?

We connect to an outdoor spigot for water — all we need is a working hose bib. No electricity required; our equipment is gas-powered.

Do I need to be home?

No. As long as we can reach water and the areas being cleaned, you don't need to be there. Interior window cleaning is the exception — someone needs to let us in.

Is it safe for my plants and pets?

Yes. The solutions we use are diluted for exterior cleaning and are safe for landscaping and animals. We just ask that pets stay inside while we're on site.

How long does a job take?

A single-service job like a driveway is usually a few hours. A full house wash with windows is a half to full day. We give you a time window when we schedule.

How and when do I pay?

We accept check, Venmo, or cash, due when the job is done. No deposit for standard jobs.

What if I'm not happy with part of the job?

Let us know and we'll come back and re-clean that area at no charge. If it's not right, we make it right.
