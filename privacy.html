Privacy Policy & Messaging Terms

Last updated August 31, 2026

Tiger Shine (doing business as Tiger Shine) ("we," "us," "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how Tiger Shine collects and uses information about you when you opt in to receive SMS messages from us.

Data we collect

When you opt in to receive SMS messages, we collect:

  • Your name
  • Your phone number
  • Consent to send SMS messages

How we use your data

We use your information to:

  • Operate our business
  • Send you the SMS messages you've opted in to receive

Data sharing

  • Customer data is not shared with third parties for promotional or marketing purposes.
  • Mobile opt-in and consent are never shared with anyone for any purpose. Any information sharing that may be mentioned elsewhere in this policy excludes mobile opt-in data.

Messaging program terms and conditions

  1. The messaging program may consist of general customer care messaging to answer questions and provide support to customers, and/or marketing messaging that includes discount codes, special deals or texts promoting our products/services, depending on which program(s) you opt in to. We will only send content you have explicitly signed up to receive via our opt-in process. Messages will be sent from 334-542-4044.
  2. You can cancel the SMS service at any time. Just text STOP to the phone number from which you received messages. After you send the SMS message STOP to us, we will send you an SMS message to confirm that you have been unsubscribed. After this, you will no longer receive SMS messages from us. If you want to join again, just sign up as you did the first time and we will start sending SMS messages to you again.
  3. If you are experiencing issues with the messaging program you can reply with the keyword HELP for more assistance, or you can get help directly at contact@tigershineservices.com.
  4. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
  5. As always, message and data rates may apply for any messages sent to you from us and to us from you. Message frequency will vary based on communication needs. If you have any questions about your text plan or data plan, it is best to contact your wireless provider.
  6. If you have any questions regarding privacy, please read the privacy policy details contained in the rest of this page or contact us at contact@tigershineservices.com.

Questions about this policy? Email contact@tigershineservices.com or call 334-542-4044.
