Good to know

Common questions

Will pressure washing damage my concrete, siding, or roof?

No — because we match the method to the surface. Hard surfaces like concrete get a surface cleaner at the right pressure; siding, brick, and painted surfaces get low-pressure “soft washing” with a cleaning solution that does the work instead of raw force.

Do you need my water or power?

Just an outdoor spigot for water — that's all we need. No electricity; our equipment is gas-powered.

How long does a typical job take?

Most driveway or single-service jobs are a few hours. A full house wash with windows is usually a half to full day. We'll give you a time window when we schedule.

How do I pay, and when?

Check, Venmo, or cash, due when the job is done. No deposit for standard jobs.
